Rules of the Game

Newsvendor Problem Instructions

You will play the role of a newspaper vendor in a game consisting of 30 rounds, with one ordering decision to be made in each round. All 30 rounds are payoff-relevant.

In each round, you must decide how many newspapers to order before knowing the actual customer demand. You may order any integer quantity.

Selling Price $12.00 per newspaper sold
Purchase Cost $3.00 per newspaper ordered

Customer demand will always be between 201 and 500 newspapers, and every integer quantity between 201 and 500 is equally likely to occur. The computer will randomly generate the demand after you submit your order.

Once demand is realized, the quantity sold and your profit for that round will be calculated automatically: $$\text{Round Profit} = \$12 \times \min(Q, D) - \$3 \times Q$$

At the end of the game, the profits from all 30 rounds will be added together to determine your total payoff. Your goal is to make effective ordering decisions and maximize your total profit over the 30 rounds.
